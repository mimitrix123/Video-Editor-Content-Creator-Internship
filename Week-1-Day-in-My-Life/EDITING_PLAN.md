# Editing Plan

## Sequence

### 1. Opening — 0:00–0:08
- Establishing morning shot.
- Title card: **DAY IN MY LIFE**.
- Fade in from black.

### 2. Morning Routine — 0:08–0:25
- Alarm → getting ready → making bed.
- Use short 2–4 second clips.
- Text: **7:00 AM — Start the day**.

### 3. Breakfast — 0:25–0:43
- Close-ups of coffee/tea and food.
- Use match cuts between preparation actions.
- Keep natural kitchen sound subtly audible beneath music.

### 4. Work / Study — 0:43–1:03
- Laptop, notebook, typing, reading.
- Cross-dissolve between location changes.
- Text: **Focus mode**.

### 5. Break / Outside — 1:03–1:22
- Walking, commute, outdoor detail shots.
- One directional/whip transition at the location change.
- Include 1–2 seconds of ambient sound for variety.

### 6. Creative Work — 1:22–1:42
- Editing, designing, coding, or another creative activity.
- Cut selected clips to the music beat.
- Avoid excessive effects; prioritize clean rhythm.

### 7. Evening — 1:42–1:54
- Sunset, exercise, reading, or relaxing.
- Use longer shots and slower pacing.

### 8. Closing — 1:54–2:00
- Final evening/desk shot.
- Text: **Thanks for watching**.
- Fade video and music to black.

## Audio Plan

- Use a properly licensed/royalty-free music track.
- Keep music around -18 to -12 dB as a starting point and adjust by ear.
- Duck music during speech or important natural audio.
- Add short fade-in and fade-out handles.

## Text Style

Keep typography consistent throughout the video. Use high contrast, short phrases, and safe margins so overlays remain readable on mobile and desktop screens.

## Export Checklist

- [ ] Runtime approximately 02:00
- [ ] 1920×1080 resolution
- [ ] 16:9 aspect ratio
- [ ] 30 fps
- [ ] H.264 MP4
- [ ] AAC audio
- [ ] No black frames or missing media
- [ ] Music is properly licensed
- [ ] Text is readable
- [ ] Audio does not clip
