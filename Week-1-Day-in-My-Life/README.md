# Mini Project — Week 1: Day in My Life Vlog

## Project Overview

A 2-minute lifestyle vlog demonstrating core video-editing and content-creation skills. The video is designed for 1080p Full HD delivery and can be produced from original clips or properly licensed stock footage.

### Requirements Completed

- 2-minute target runtime
- 1080p Full HD export
- Stock footage / original clips workflow
- Clean cuts and pacing
- Transitions between major scenes
- Background music with controlled volume
- Text overlays and captions
- Opening title card
- Closing end card
- Structured project documentation

## Storyboard / Timeline

| Time | Scene | Visual | Edit / Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:08 | Title | Morning exterior / bedroom | Fade in + title: **DAY IN MY LIFE** |
| 00:08–00:25 | Morning | Alarm, waking up, making bed | Quick cuts + overlay: **7:00 AM — Start the day** |

| 01:42–01:54 | Evening | Sunset / exercise / relaxation | Slow cuts + warm pacing |
| 01:54–02:00 | Closing | Evening city/desk shot | Fade out + **Thanks for watching** |

## Editing Specifications

- **Resolution:** 1920 × 1080 (1080p)
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9
- **Frame rate:** 30 fps
- **Target duration:** 02:00
- **Format:** MP4
- **Video codec:** H.264
- **Audio codec:** AAC
- **Suggested audio level:** music kept below dialogue/important natural sound

## Recommended Editing Workflow

1. Create a 1920×1080, 30 fps project.
2. Import all video clips, music, and graphics.
3. Organize footage into `Morning`, `Work`, `Break`, `Evening`, and `Graphics` bins.
4. Select the strongest moments and assemble the rough cut according to the timeline above.
5. Trim pauses and repetitive footage to keep the pacing engaging.
6. Add simple transitions (cuts, dissolves, and one directional transition) without overusing effects.
7. Add the title card during the first 8 seconds.
8. Add short text overlays to establish time, activity, and mood.
9. Add royalty-free background music and duck it whenever speech or important natural sound is present.
10. Apply basic color correction so clips have consistent exposure and white balance.
11. Add the closing card and fade audio/video out smoothly.
12. Export as 1080p H.264 MP4 and verify the final runtime is approximately 2 minutes.
