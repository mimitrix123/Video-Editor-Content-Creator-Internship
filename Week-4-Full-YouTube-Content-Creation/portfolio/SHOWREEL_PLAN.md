# Professional Video Editing Showreel Plan

## Goal

Create a polished 60–90 second portfolio reel that demonstrates editing range, storytelling, short-form pacing, graphics, color, and sound design.

## Sequence

| Time | Segment | Showcase |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Opening | Name/logo + strongest shot |
| 0:05–0:25 | Fast montage | Cuts, transitions, captions |
| 0:25–0:45 | Long-form | Storytelling, B-roll, talking-head editing |
| 0:45–1:05 | Short-form | Shorts/Reels pacing and vertical edits |
| 1:05–1:20 | Technical | Graphics, color, sound design |
| 1:20–1:30 | Contact | Name + portfolio/contact card |

## Editing Style

- Cut on action and natural audio beats.
- Use transitions selectively.
- Show before/after comparisons when possible.
- Keep text minimal and readable.
- Use sound effects to support visual changes.
- Maintain a consistent visual identity.
