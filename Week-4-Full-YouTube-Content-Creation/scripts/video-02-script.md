# Video 02 Script

## How to Edit a Professional YouTube Video From Start to Finish

**Target:** 4–6 minutes

### Hook — 00:00–00:15

Here’s how I would take a folder of raw clips and turn it into a polished YouTube video—from organising the footage to the final export.

### Organise — 00:15–00:40

First, separate footage into A-roll, B-roll, audio, graphics, music, and exports. Good organisation saves time throughout the entire edit.

### Rough Cut — 00:40–01:20

Build the story using the strongest takes first. Remove mistakes and dead space before adding effects. The goal is to make the story work even without music or graphics.

### B-Roll & Pacing — 01:20–02:00

Cover cuts with relevant B-roll and vary shot sizes. Use punch-ins and occasional speed changes only where they improve the rhythm.

### Audio — 02:00–02:45

Clean dialogue first, then balance music and effects underneath it. Listen on headphones and speakers so the voice remains easy to understand.

### Color — 02:45–03:30

Correct exposure and white balance before applying a creative look. Keep skin tones natural and make shots from different cameras feel consistent.

### Graphics — 03:30–04:15

Add titles, call-outs, captions, and a consistent lower-third style. Keep animations short and readable.

### Final Review — 04:15–04:45

Watch the complete video without editing. Check pacing, audio peaks, spelling, graphics, transitions, and continuity.

### CTA — 04:45–05:00

Export the final version, upload it with a strong title and thumbnail, and use the description to help viewers understand exactly what they’ll learn.
