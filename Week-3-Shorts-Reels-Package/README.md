# Mini Project — Week 3: YouTube Shorts / Reels Package

## Project Overview

Create a short-form content package containing **3 vertical videos**, each 15–60 seconds long, optimized for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and similar mobile-first platforms.

The package demonstrates short-form storytelling, caption design, music synchronization, zoom effects, speed ramps, visual consistency, and thumbnail design.

## Video Concepts

### Video 01 — Quick Tip
**Topic:** One useful editing/content-creation tip.

**Structure:**
- 0:00–0:03 — Hook: “Stop doing this in your videos!”
- 0:03–0:12 — Show the common mistake.
- 0:12–0:25 — Demonstrate the better technique.
- 0:25–0:35 — Before/after comparison.
- 0:35–0:40 — CTA: “Follow for more editing tips.”

**Editing:** punch-in zooms on key moments, caption emphasis, one speed ramp at the reveal, and music synced to cuts.

### Video 02 — Before / After
**Topic:** Show a raw clip transformed through editing.

**Structure:**
- 0:00–0:04 — “RAW vs EDITED” hook.
- 0:04–0:15 — Raw footage.
- 0:15–0:32 — Editing process montage.
- 0:32–0:45 — Final result.
- 0:45–0:50 — CTA.

**Editing:** split-screen or wipe comparison, speed ramp through the editing montage, zoom on details, and bold captions.

### Video 03 — Fast Tutorial
**Topic:** A simple technique viewers can replicate.

**Structure:**
- 0:00–0:03 — Outcome-first hook.
- 0:03–0:10 — Step 1.
- 0:10–0:20 — Step 2.
- 0:20–0:30 — Step 3.
- 0:30–0:40 — Final result.
- 0:40–0:45 — “Save this for later” CTA.

**Editing:** numbered captions, controlled zoom effects, speed ramp between steps, and music beat cuts.

## Required Editing Features

### Captions
- Generate or transcribe captions accurately.
- Keep lines short and readable on mobile.
- Highlight important words with consistent styling.
- Keep captions within safe margins and away from platform UI areas.

### Trending Music
- Select music that is currently appropriate for the platform and audience.
- Use music only where the license/platform usage rights permit it.
- Keep music underneath speech.
- Cut or transition on musical beats when appropriate.

### Zoom Effects
- Use keyframed digital punch-ins to emphasize reactions, details, or important text.
- Avoid excessive zooming that makes footage uncomfortable to watch.

### Speed Ramps
- Use speed changes to compress repetitive actions or emphasize transitions.
- Add motion blur only when supported and visually appropriate.
- Ensure speed changes remain smooth and intentional.

## Vertical Format

- **Resolution:** 1080 × 1920
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16
- **Frame rate:** 30 fps recommended
- **Format:** MP4
- **Video codec:** H.264
- **Audio codec:** AAC
- **Duration:** 15–60 seconds per video

## Thumbnail Design System

Create one matching thumbnail for each short. Maintain a consistent visual identity while changing the subject/text to match each video.

### Thumbnail Formula

**Main subject + 2–5 word headline + strong visual focal point**

Examples:
- **VIDEO 01:** “EDIT FASTER”
- **VIDEO 02:** “RAW → PRO”
- **VIDEO 03:** “3 EASY STEPS”

Design requirements:
- 16:9 thumbnail canvas for the platform's standard thumbnail representation.
- Large readable headline.
- Strong subject image or frame from the corresponding video.
- Consistent typography and branding across all three thumbnails.
- High contrast and simple composition.
