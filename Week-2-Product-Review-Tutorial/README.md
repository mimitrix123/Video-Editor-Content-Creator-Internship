# Mini Project — Week 2: Product Review / Tutorial Video

## Project Overview

A 3-minute product review/tutorial designed to demonstrate professional editing, narration, audio mixing, visual call-outs, color correction, and audience-focused CTA design.

**Suggested example:** a review/tutorial of a smartphone, laptop accessory, camera, productivity app, or other everyday product.

## Project Goals

- Create a strong hook in the first 10–15 seconds.
- Use clear voiceover narration to guide the viewer.
- Support narration with B-roll and close-up product shots.
- Add readable text call-outs for important features and specifications.
- Apply consistent color correction and basic grading.
- Use background music without overpowering narration.
- Finish with a clear **Subscribe / Follow for more** CTA screen.

## Sample Voiceover Script

> **Hook:** “Is this product actually worth your money? I tested it in everyday use, and here are the three things you should know before buying.”
>
> **Intro:** “Welcome back. In this quick review, we're looking at the product, its most useful features, how it performs in real-world use, and whether I would recommend it.”
>
> **Features:** “First, let's look at the design and the features that stand out. The main controls are easy to access, while the overall setup is straightforward. On screen, I'll highlight the specifications that matter most.”
>
> **Testing:** “Now let's see how it performs outside the box. During normal use, the most noticeable strengths are ease of use and consistency. Here's a closer look at the feature I found most useful.”
>
> **Pros and Cons:** “The biggest pros are the straightforward setup, useful features, and overall usability. The main drawbacks are the limitations shown here, so keep those in mind before purchasing.”
>
> **Verdict:** “Overall, this is a solid choice if these features match what you need. If you value simplicity and practical performance, it is worth considering.”
>
> **CTA:** “If this review helped you, subscribe for more practical reviews and tutorials, and leave a comment with the product you want to see next.”

## Editing Workflow

1. Create a 1920×1080, 30 fps timeline.
2. Import A-roll, B-roll, voiceover, music, graphics, and sound effects.
3. Build the hook first using the strongest 2–4 clips.
4. Record or import clean voiceover narration and remove obvious pauses/noise.
5. Cut B-roll over narration so every visual supports what is being said.
6. Add text call-outs for specifications, features, measurements, prices, or key observations.
7. Apply basic color correction: exposure, white balance, contrast, and consistent skin/product tones.
8. Add subtle transitions only where they improve continuity.
9. Mix audio so narration remains clear and music sits underneath it.
10. Create a branded end screen with a subscribe CTA, channel/creator name, and optional next-video placeholder.
11. Review the full timeline for pacing, spelling, audio clipping, and visual consistency.
12. Export the final video as an H.264 MP4.

## Color Correction Checklist

- Match exposure between clips.
- Correct white balance before adjusting creative look.
- Recover highlights where possible.
- Lift crushed shadows carefully.
- Keep product colors accurate.
- Apply the same overall look across shots.

## Audio Mix Checklist

- Voiceover is the primary element.
- Remove distracting background noise.
- Use compression/EQ as needed for consistent speech.
- Keep music clearly below narration.
- Add short audio fades between sections.
- Check the final mix on headphones and speakers.

## Text Call-Out Examples

- **Battery:** “Up to XX hours”
- **Display:** “XX-inch / XX Hz”
- **Feature:** “Fast setup”
- **Verdict:** “Best for everyday use”
- **Rating:** “8/10”

Only include factual specifications that have been verified from the product or an authoritative source.
